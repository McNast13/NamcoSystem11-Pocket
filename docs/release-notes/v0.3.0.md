**What's new in v0.3.0**

- **Analog stick works as a d-pad.** On a Dock controller with an analog stick, pushing the left stick more than about halfway
  also presses the d-pad, diagonals included, so Tekken, Tekken 2, Soul Edge and the other d-pad games can be played with the
  stick. This applies to players 1 and 2, and the d-pad works as before.
  - Point Blank 2 and Gunbarl still aim proportionally with the stick, as in v0.2.1.
  - Handheld play is unchanged: the Pocket has no analog stick.

Everything from v0.2.2 is included: game settings saved between launches, and core settings (DIP1 Test, DIP2 Freeze, Service
button, Gun aim speed) that take effect.
