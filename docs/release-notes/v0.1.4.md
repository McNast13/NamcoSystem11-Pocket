**What's new in v0.1.4**

- **No line at the top of the picture on the Pocket's screen.** A short line showed above or at the top of the picture on the
  handheld (top right in Tekken and Tekken 2, top left in Soul Edge); the Dock was not affected. The core sent the Pocket's
  scaler one extra black line before each frame and the scaler only partly wrote it. Frames now go out without it and always
  with an even number of lines, as in version 0.5.6 of the PlayStation core.
- **Pocket Racer can be steered with the d-pad.** On the Pocket's own controls and on controllers without analog sticks, the
  car could not be steered and the menus did not respond: the core read those controllers' unused sticks as held at full
  lock. Sticks are now read only from controllers that have them. Left/right on the d-pad steers (a virtual wheel that turns
  while held and re-centres when let go), and an analog stick on the Dock still steers too.

Tested on a Pocket, handheld and docked: Tekken, Tekken 2, Soul Edge, Xevious 3D/G and Pocket Racer.
